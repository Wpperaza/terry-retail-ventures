How we choose

A category has to earn its place

These are the areas we actively buy and evaluate in — not a fixed catalog. What we carry inside any category depends on the suppliers behind it and on whether we can list, ship and support the product properly.

Before we open a new category we look at steady demand rather than spikes, whether supply can be documented, packaging and shipping requirements, and any brand or channel restrictions that apply.

What we will not list

  • Products we cannot trace to a documented supply source
  • Items whose condition or packaging we cannot describe accurately
  • Lines that conflict with a brand's stated channel policy
  • Categories requiring handling or certifications outside our current scope
