Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information Terry Pet Lovers LLC, doing business as Terry Retail Ventures ("we", "us"), a Florida limited liability company based in Davenport, Florida, collects through terryretailventures.com and how we use it.

What we collect

We collect only what you send us through the contact or application forms or by email or phone: your name, company, work email, phone number, website, and the message or product details you include. We do not sell products on this site, and we do not collect payment card information here.

How we use it

We use your information to read and answer your message, to evaluate a possible supplier or brand relationship, and to keep a record of our business correspondence. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to us by Web3Forms, a form service that passes your message to our email inbox. Our email is hosted by Google. The site is delivered through Cloudflare, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so those providers may see technical data such as your IP address when you visit. We share information only with providers that help us run the site and with anyone the law requires us to.

Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as it is useful for our business or required for our records, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information you sent us by emailing info@terryretailventures.com.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
