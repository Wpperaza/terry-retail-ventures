Why brands can approve us with confidence.

These are commitments we make to every brand we work with. If a policy of yours is stricter, tell us and we follow yours.

  • MAP compliance.We advertise at or above your minimum advertised price, and we do not discount below it.
  • Authorized sourcing only.We buy from the brand or from distributors the brand has authorized. No gray-market goods.
  • No repackaged or unauthorized product.We do not relabel, repack, alter identifiers or resell expired, recalled or improperly stored goods.
  • Brand protection.We keep purchase invoices and honor resale restrictions. If you tell us a product or channel is off limits, we stop.
  • Listing quality.Titles, images, UPCs and product details follow the information you provide, and we do not edit your claims.

Where we sell

We sell online. Our main channel is Amazon.com, through our storefront, Terry Retail Ventures. If your brand has rules for online or marketplace sales, we want to know them before we place an order, and we will ask for your written approval where your terms require it.

Payment

Prepaid or ACH. No credit contracts.

What we ask from you

  • Line card or catalog
  • Wholesale price list, with tier or volume breaks
  • Case pack and minimum opening order
  • UPC or EAN, dimensions, weights and usable images
  • Lead times and current stock
  • Your resale and MAP policy
