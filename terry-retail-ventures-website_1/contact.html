Direct contact

Two ways to reach us

Vendor, wholesale and purchasing matters go to our purchasing address so they reach the right person first time. Everything else goes to our general address.

  • Purchasing & vendors purchasing@terryretailventures.com Catalogs, price lists, MOQ and case packs, account applications, brand and channel matters.
  • General inquiries info@terryretailventures.com Anything that is not purchasing, including privacy and website questions.
  • Location Central Florida, United States

Response times. Purchasing inquiries are reviewed by a person, not a queue. We aim to reply promptly and will tell you plainly if a line is not a fit.
