A family business in Florida, since 2024.

Terry Retail Ventures is the trade name of Terry Pet Lovers LLC, a Florida limited liability company based in Davenport. A team of three runs it, all family.

We buy consumer products at wholesale from brands and authorized distributors, and sell them online. Pet supplies are our category today. We are expanding into tools and outdoor equipment, one brand at a time.

How we buy

  • We buy from the source.Directly from the brand or from a distributor the brand has authorized.
  • We represent products accurately.Titles, identifiers, condition, quantities and packaging match what you ship.
  • We pay on your terms, up front.Prepaid or ACH. No credit lines and no consignment.
  • We plan inventory responsibly.First orders are sized to what we can service. We reorder what sells.
  • We respect your policies.MAP, resale restrictions and channel rules.
