About Terry Retail Ventures

A Florida retail and purchasing business, operated by Terry Pet Lovers LLC.

Who we are

We buy well, list accurately, and stand behind what we sell

Terry Retail Ventures is a business identity operated by Terry Pet Lovers LLC, a Florida limited liability company. We operate as a multichannel retail business: we purchase consumer products at wholesale and sell them through the online retail channels we operate.

We build dependable purchasing relationships with manufacturers, distributors, and established wholesale partners. That means clear communication, orders that arrive as agreed, invoices settled on time, and product information handled with the same care the supplier put into it.

Our buying discipline is straightforward. We stock what we can source with proper documentation, describe correctly, and support after the sale — and we pass on the rest, however attractive the margin looks.

What guides our buying

Six standards we apply to every purchase

Verified sourcing

We buy from suppliers who can show where their stock originates. Unclear supply chains are a decline, not a negotiation.

Accurate representation

Titles, identifiers, condition, quantities and packaging are described exactly as the product ships.

Responsible inventory planning

Purchase quantities are evaluated against demand, product requirements, and responsible inventory planning.

Payment as agreed

Invoices are settled on the terms we agree. Reliable payment is the least a supplier should expect.

Policy and IP respect

We follow brand, channel and pricing policies where they apply, and steer clear of anything with intellectual-property risk.

Relationships over transactions

We would rather become a steady account with a supplier who fits than buy once and disappear.

How a purchase actually happens

  1. We identify a category gap or a supplier opportunity worth testing.
  2. We review costs, packaging, restrictions and how the product would be listed.
  3. We place a first order sized to what we can service properly.
  4. We track sell-through, then reorder and widen the assortment where it works.
How we work

A consistent, disciplined purchasing process

The business is designed so that no single product, supplier or sales channel carries it. Each category is evaluated the same way, and a supplier who performs well simply gets more of our purchasing.

It also means we are candid about fit. If a line is not right for our channels, we will say so quickly rather than leave a quote sitting open.
