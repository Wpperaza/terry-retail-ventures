What we do

Sourcing, listing and fulfilment handled with care

We keep the buying side of our business deliberately simple, so suppliers always know what to expect from us.

Considered sourcing

We buy from manufacturers, distributors and wholesale suppliers who can document where their stock comes from.

Accurate listings

Every product is described honestly, with correct identifiers, condition and packaging details.

Responsible inventory

Purchase quantities are evaluated against demand, product requirements, and responsible inventory planning.

Relationships that repeat

We would rather reorder steadily from a supplier who fits than chase one-off deals.
