# Review Before Launch — factual placeholders

Every statement of fact that appears on the public site, and what you need to confirm about each one. Nothing on the site claims volumes, revenue, years in business, staff counts, named brands, certifications or testimonials — and nothing announces what the business does not yet have. That keeps this list short.

---

## 2. Commitments you are making to suppliers

These are promises rather than facts. They cost nothing today, but a supplier may hold you to them — confirm you are comfortable with each.

| # | Commitment | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | We can supply **business documentation, entity verification and tax documentation** for a vendor account application | Vendor Partnerships, "What we provide"; home trust list | 
| 9 | We are **set up to prepay initial orders** by company card or bank transfer | Vendor Partnerships, "Payment" |
| 10 | We can confirm a **designated ship-to / receiving location** at account setup | Vendor Partnerships, "Shipping & receiving" and "What we provide" |
| 11 | We **follow MAP, brand and channel policies**, and will remove a line promptly if a brand asks | Vendor Partnerships, "Brand policy"; About values |
| 12 | We **decline** gray-market, expired, repackaged, altered-identifier, recalled and specially-regulated goods | Vendor Partnerships, "What we decline" |

| 14 | Vendor documentation is **shared privately, never published** on the site | Vendor Partnerships |

> Note on #8: the site never publishes your EIN, resale certificate, bank details or any document — it only says documentation is available during an application, which is standard and safe.
>
> Note on #9 and #10: if you do not yet have a receiving arrangement or a business card/transfer method ready for a first order, soften or remove those two paragraphs before launch rather than leaving them unqualified.

## 3. Product categories listed

The site lists eight categories: pet supplies, home and household, personal care, beauty, automotive accessories, office products, electronics accessories, general merchandise.

The pages state plainly that these are **areas we buy and evaluate in, not a guaranteed catalog**, so no listing is implied. Drop any category you do not actually want supplier approaches for — the cards are in `categories.html`, the first four also appear on `index.html`, and the same list drives the checkboxes on the contact form.

## 4. Explicitly NOT claimed anywhere (keep it that way)

- No years in business, founding date or company history
- No revenue, order volume, unit counts or purchasing capacity
- No customer, employee or vendor counts
- No named brands, brand logos or vendor logos
- No authorized-reseller, distributor or brand-representative status is claimed; the site simply states that authorizations are established through written agreements where applicable
- No certifications, memberships or awards
- No testimonials, reviews or ratings
- No street address, phone number, EIN, resale certificate or banking detail
- No mention of any specific online marketplace by name

## 5. Once these become true, they are worth adding

Add them only when you can document them: real vendor references (with permission), a genuine business milestone, a certification actually obtained, and a resale certificate number where a vendor form requires it — shared privately, never published on the site.
